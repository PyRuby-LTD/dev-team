# Reusable products, sprint zero and specialists

Named later theme: turn a product brief and constraints into a walking skeleton with infrastructure/pipeline planning, sprint-zero backlog and product-specific specialist extensions. Revisit after the first journey is proven and another product supplies reuse requirements. No budgets, hosting defaults or mandatory specialist roster are inferred.

## Success criteria

- [human] Given a new product brief and constraints, when sprint-zero scope is proposed, then the customer can inspect its walking skeleton, infrastructure, pipeline and backlog before play. Evidence: future reviewed sample engagement.
- [automated] Given an optional specialist extension, when its workflow is validated, then each step still has one owner and no collaborators field. Evidence: future extension contract fixtures.
