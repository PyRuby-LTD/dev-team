# EPIC-002 Closed beta onboarding

**Depth: detailed**

## Vision
Twenty Reading Road Runners members have their activities syncing, set up with
hand-holding rather than self-service.

## Value
Proves the transport works for people who are not its author - different
watches, different firmware, different tolerance for setup - before any
commitment to commercial onboarding.

## Scope
Repeatable manual setup, and whatever support burden that reveals. Onboarding
may be unpolished by agreement.

## Success criteria
- Twenty members set up without the author writing bespoke code per person
- The setup can be performed by following written instructions
- Support requests over a month are countable and their causes known

## Out of scope
Self-serve signup, and replacing the personal access token step. Both deferred
to commercial launch - see `scope.md`.
